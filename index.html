I’m Ifeoluwa, a Clarity & Purpose Coach and a registered nurse. In my work I’ve seen that many people don’t lack ability. They lack clarity. They feel stuck, restless, or lost, and can’t say why.

My faith is the root of how I coach. I believe direction is not something you chase alone. It comes through honest reflection, prayer, and wise conversation.

Today I walk with people through those conversations until the fog lifts and their next step is clear. My goal is simple: to help you find your next yes.
